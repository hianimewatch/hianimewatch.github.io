Subbed or dubbed anime

Subbed anime keeps the original Japanese voices and adds English subtitles. Dubbed anime has English voice acting. Many popular series have both, and the dub is listed as its own title, for example One Piece (Dub) next to One Piece. If you are new to anime, subs usually keep the original performances, while dubs let you watch without reading.

Watch order guides: skip the filler

Long series have filler episodes that do not follow the manga. These guides show which arcs to watch, and which to skip, so you can watch anime online without wasting time.

One Piece watch order (1,100+ episodes)

One Piece has one of the lowest filler rates in anime, under 10 percent. The main sagas are:

  • CANONEast Blue (eps 1-61). Introduces Zoro, Nami, Usopp and Sanji. Skip the Warship Island filler, eps 54-61.
  • CANONAlabasta (eps 62-135). Chopper joins on Drum Island, then the fight against Crocodile. Skip eps 131-143.
  • CANONSkypiea (eps 136-206). Jaya and Enel's kingdom in the clouds. Keep the G-8 arc, eps 196-206, which fans rate highly.
  • CANONWater 7 and Enies Lobby (eps 207-325). CP9, Robin's past and the declaration of war on the World Government.
  • CANONThriller Bark to Marineford (eps 326-516). Brook joins, then Sabaody, Impel Down and the Summit War.
  • CANONNew World (eps 517 onward). Fish-Man Island, Punk Hazard, Dressrosa, Whole Cake Island, Wano and the Egghead arc.

Bleach and Thousand-Year Blood War without filler

The original Bleach has about 45 percent filler. Skipping those arcs takes you smoothly to Thousand-Year Blood War, which has no filler.

  • CANONSoul Society (eps 1-63). Ichigo becomes a Soul Reaper and goes after Rukia.
  • SKIPBount arc (eps 64-109). Non-canon filler.
  • CANONArrancar and Hueco Mundo (eps 110-167, 190-205). Skip the Amagai filler, eps 168-189.
  • CANONFake Karakura Town (eps 215-310). The battle with Aizen. Skip the Zanpakuto Rebellion filler, eps 230-265.
  • CANONLost Agent (eps 343-366). The canon bridge to the final arc.
  • WATCHThousand-Year Blood War. Part 1, then Part 2: The Separation.

Most series are available both with the original Japanese audio and English subtitles and as an English dub. Dubbed versions are listed as separate titles, usually marked "(Dub)", and you can switch between them from the episode page.
